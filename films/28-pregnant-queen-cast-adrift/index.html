The pregnant queen is cast adrift

c. 1388

English and Assamese subtitles provide scene context, rather than transcribed speech.

Scene description

While King Tyao Khamti is away battling Chutias, his jealous Chief Queen falsely accuses the beloved younger queen and sentences her to be cast adrift on a raft down the Brahmaputra.

The paintings, portraits and films are modern reconstructions. Dialogue is dramatised; source records may disagree.
