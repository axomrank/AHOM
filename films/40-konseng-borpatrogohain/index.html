Konseng becomes Barpatragohain

c. 1506

English and Assamese subtitles provide scene context, rather than transcribed speech.

Scene description

Suhungmung creates a third great ministerial post—the Barpatragohain—elevating Konseng to equal rank with Burhagohain and Borgohain.

Read the historical scene and sources

The paintings, portraits and films are modern reconstructions. Dialogue is dramatised; source records may disagree.
