Sukaphaa feasts with the Moran and Borahi

1235–1240

English and Assamese subtitles provide scene context, rather than transcribed speech.

Scene description

Sukaphaa adopts an assimilative policy by establishing matrimonial bonds with the indigenous Moran chief Bodousa and the Borahi king Thakumtha, marrying local princesses and integrating them into court governance.

The paintings, portraits and films are modern reconstructions. Dialogue is dramatised; source records may disagree.
