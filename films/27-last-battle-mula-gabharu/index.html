Mula Gabharu’s last battle

1532–1533

English and Assamese subtitles provide scene context, rather than transcribed speech.

Scene description

After her husband Phrasengmung Borgohain is killed by Turbak's army, Princess Mula Gabharu leads women warriors into battle and is killed with five companions. Her death rallies the Ahom army.

Read the historical scene and sources

The paintings, portraits and films are modern reconstructions. Dialogue is dramatised; source records may disagree.
