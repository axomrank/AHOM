Chao Pulai’s secret pact with Kamata

c. 1335

English and Assamese subtitles provide scene context, rather than transcribed speech.

Scene description

Chao Pulai disputes the succession of his half-brother Sukhrangphaa and conspires with maternal Kamata envoys for military support.
