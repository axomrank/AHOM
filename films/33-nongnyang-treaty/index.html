The boundary treaty at Nongnyang Lake

1401

English and Assamese subtitles provide scene context, rather than transcribed speech.

Scene description

Ahom and Mong Kawng envoys swear a treaty by Nongnyang lake fixing the Patkai range as the border between the two kingdoms. The boundary holds for centuries.

The paintings, portraits and films are modern reconstructions. Dialogue is dramatised; source records may disagree.
