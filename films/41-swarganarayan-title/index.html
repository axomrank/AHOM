Suhungmung adopts the title Swarganarayan

c. 1510

English and Assamese subtitles provide scene context, rather than transcribed speech.

Scene description

Suhungmung officially assumes the Sanskrit title 'Swarganarayan' (Lord of Heaven), introducing the Saka calendar into state epigraphy.
