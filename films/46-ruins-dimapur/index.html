Soldiers patrol the ruins of Dimapur

c. 1536

English and Assamese subtitles provide scene context, rather than transcribed speech.

Scene description

The vassal Kachari king Detsung revolts. The Ahoms pursue him to Jangmarang and kill him; Dimapur is abandoned and the Kacharis move their capital to Maibong. Ahom rule now reaches the Kolong river.

Read the historical scene and sources

The paintings, portraits and films are modern reconstructions. Dialogue is dramatised; source records may disagree.
