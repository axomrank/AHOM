Sukhaangphaa weds Rajani of Kamata

c. 1308

English and Assamese subtitles provide scene context, rather than transcribed speech.

Scene description

Peace concluded by the marriage of Kamata Princess Rajani (Durlabhanarayana's daughter) to Sukhangphaa, sealing durable inter-kingdom peace.
