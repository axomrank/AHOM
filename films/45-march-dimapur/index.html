The army marches toward Dimapur

1526–1531

English and Assamese subtitles provide scene context, rather than transcribed speech.

Scene description

After five years of war, the Kachari king Khunkhara's army is beaten and its commander killed. The Ahoms chase him to his capital Dimapur; he flees, and Suhungmung installs Detsung as Kachari king.

Read the historical scene and sources

The paintings, portraits and films are modern reconstructions. Dialogue is dramatised; source records may disagree.
