Prince Sudangphaa is born at Habung

1388

English and Assamese subtitles provide scene context, rather than transcribed speech.

Scene description

The banished pregnant queen washes ashore at Habung and is sheltered by a pious Brahmin family; she gives birth to prince Sudang before passing away.

The paintings, portraits and films are modern reconstructions. Dialogue is dramatised; source records may disagree.
