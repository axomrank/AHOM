Sukaphaa in the new rice fields

c. 1240–1250

English and Assamese subtitles provide scene context, rather than transcribed speech.

Scene description

Introduction of advanced Tai hydraulic techniques, dyke bunding, canal irrigation, and deep-water transplanted Sali rice, producing an agricultural surplus.

The paintings, portraits and films are modern reconstructions. Dialogue is dramatised; source records may disagree.
