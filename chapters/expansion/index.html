Swarganarayan’s kingdom

1497–1603

Suhungmung, the first Ahom king to take a Hindu title, Swarganarayan, doubled the kingdom. He annexed the Chutia lands in 1523, drove the Kacharis back to Dimapur and turned back the armies of Bengal.

When the Bengal general Turbak invaded in 1532, Mula Gabharu took up her fallen husband’s sword and died fighting. Later in 1533 the Ahoms killed Turbak and chased his army back to the Karatoya. Suklenmung built Garhgaon as a fortified capital, and Sukhaamphaa held off the Koch general Chilarai in 1562.
