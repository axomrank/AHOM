The Moamoria risings

1769–1817

In 1769 the Moamorias took Rangpur and imprisoned Lakshmi Singha. Queen Kuranganayani had the rebel leader killed and the king restored. The second rising, in 1786, drove Gaurinath Singha down the Brahmaputra to Guwahati.

The king asked the East India Company for help, and Captain Welsh’s sepoys pushed the rebels back between 1792 and 1794. The capital moved to Jorhat, Purnananda Burhagohain governed in the king’s name, and the kingdom never recovered its old strength.
