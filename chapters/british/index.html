The last Swargadeo

1826–1838

The Ahom nobles had expected their kingdom back. Instead Gomdhar Konwar rose in 1828, and in 1830 Piyali Phukan and Jiuram Dulia Baruah were hanged for plotting to drive the British out.

In 1833 the Company made Purandar Singha a tributary king of Upper Assam and set a tribute he could not pay. In 1838 it removed him and annexed the land, six hundred and ten years after Sukaphaa crossed the Patkai.
