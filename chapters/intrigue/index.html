Six kings in nine years

1672–1681

After Saraighat the ministers held the real power. Debera Borbarua made kings and murdered them; Atan Burhagohain overthrew him; Laluk Sola Borphukan handed Guwahati back to the Mughals in 1679.

To keep a boy-king on the throne, Laluk had the princes maimed, since a maimed prince could never rule. Prince Gadapani escaped into the hills. His wife Joymoti was tortured to death at Jerenga in 1680 rather than say where he was.
