The Tungkhungia kings

1681–1769

Gadapani came out of hiding as Gadadhar Singha, drove the Mughals past the Manas in 1682 and began the Tungkhungia line. His son Rudra Singha built Rangpur and the Joysagar tank and made the Kachari and Jaintia kings his vassals.

Under Siva Singha three queens ruled in turn as Bar Raja. Phuleswari’s persecution of the Moamoria sattra planted the next century’s revolt. Pramatta Singha raised the Rang Ghar in brick, and Rajeswar Singha rebuilt the Kareng Ghar at Garhgaon.
