The days of the Burmese

1817–1826

Badan Chandra Borphukan, fleeing his enemies at court, brought a Burmese army into Assam in 1817. Three invasions followed. Chandrakanta, Purandar, Chandrakanta again, then Jogeswar as a puppet: kings were set up and thrown down while the Burmese commander Mingimaha Tilwa held the valley.

When Britain and Burma went to war in 1824, British troops drove the Burmese out. The Treaty of Yandabo, signed in February 1826, gave Assam to the East India Company.
