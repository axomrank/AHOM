Against the Mughal Empire

1603–1681

Pratap Singha and his minister Momai Tamuli Borbarua rebuilt the paik system and created the offices of Borbarua and Borphukan. After years of war the Mughals accepted a frontier on the Barnadi in 1639.

In 1662 Mir Jumla took Garhgaon, but the monsoon and the hill war around Namrup wore his army down, and he died on the way back. Chakradhwaj Singha would not live with the treaty’s shame. His commander Lachit Borphukan retook Guwahati in 1667 and in 1671 broke Ram Singh’s fleet at Saraighat.
