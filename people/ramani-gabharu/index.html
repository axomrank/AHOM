Ramani Gabharu

ৰমণী গাভৰু

1663 · Princess sent to the Mughals

Sent to the Mughal court under the 1663 Treaty of Ghilajharighat. Sources differ on whether she was Jayadhwaj Singha's daughter or Lachit's niece.

Record confidence: low.
