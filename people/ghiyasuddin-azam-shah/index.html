Ghiyasuddin Azam Shah

c. 1396–1400 (reign 1389–1410) · Sultan of Bengal

An inscription at Boko in Kamrup shows the Sultanate held the region for some years. The Kamata king, allied with the Ahoms by marriage, drove him back across the Karatoya.

Dates are inferred from coins.

Record confidence: medium.
