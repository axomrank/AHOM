Lakshmi Singha (Sunyeophaa)

লক্ষ্মী সিংহ

1769–1780 · Thirty-fourth Ahom king

Made king by Kirti Chandra Borbarua. The Moamoria rebellion began in 1769 and the rebels captured him; royalists restored him in 1770.

Died 13 December 1780.
