Pratap Singha (Susenghphaa)

প্ৰতাপ সিংহ

1603–1641 · Seventeenth Ahom king

Created the Borbarua and Borphukan offices, reorganised the paik system through Momai Tamuli Borbarua, and fought the first Mughal wars.

Also known as Burha Roja and Buddhiswarganarayan. Married Mangaldahi, daughter of Parikshit Narayan, in 1608.
