Phuleswari (Pramatheswari)

ফুলেশ্বৰী

Bar Raja 1722–1731 · Queen who ruled as Bar Raja

A dancer of the Nat caste made Bar Raja under Siva Singha after astrologers predicted the king's fall. Minted coins in joint names and pressed to make Shaktism the state religion; died in 1731 in childbirth.

Her insult to the Moamoria Gosains is cited as a cause of the later rebellion.

Record confidence: high.
