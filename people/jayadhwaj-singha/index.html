Jayadhwaj Singha (Sutamla)

জয়ধ্বজ সিংহ

1648–1663 · Twentieth Ahom king

Drove the Mughals from Guwahati in 1648. Mir Jumla took Garhgaon in March 1662; the king withdrew to Namrup and signed the Treaty of Ghilajharighat in January 1663. A patron of Vaishnava satras.

Called Bhagania Roja (“the fugitive”). Struck the first coins in his name.
