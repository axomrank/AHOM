Sudangphaa (Bamuni Konwar)

সুদাংফা

1397–1407 · Eighth Ahom king

Son of Tyao Khamti, raised by a Brahmin family in Habung after his mother's execution; found and enthroned at about fifteen in 1397. He introduced the Singarigharutha coronation (1403) and let Hindu rites into the court.

Peace treaty fixing the Patkai boundary in 1401; capital moved to Charguwa in 1403.
