Bhagyachandra (Jai Singh)

1748–1798 · King of Manipur

Fled to Ahom territory after the Burmese invasion of 1764 and was sheltered by Rajeswar Singha, who backed his return; reinstated in 1773. Promoted Vaishnavism and the Manipuri Raas Leela.

Record confidence: high.
