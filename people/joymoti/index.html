Joymoti Konwari

জয়মতী কোঁৱৰী

d. 1680 · Ahom princess and wife of Gadadhar Singha

Wife of the prince who became king Gadadhar Singha. Tortured and killed by agents of Sulikphaa and Laluk Sola Borphukan while her husband hid in exile.

Record confidence: medium.
