Captain Francis Jenkins

1835–1838 · British Commissioner and Agent to the Governor-General

Investigated Purandar Singha's administration in 1835–38 and reported it as a hotbed of corruption; Purandar was deposed and the territory annexed in September 1838.

After the 1826 cut-off; included because it ends the Purandar Singha story.

Record confidence: medium.
