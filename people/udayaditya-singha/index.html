Udayaditya Singha (Sunyatphaa)

উদয়াদিত্য সিংহ

1670–1672 · Twenty-second Ahom king

Brother of Chakradhwaj; renewed the war with the Mughals and ordered Lachit to Saraighat. Deposed and executed.
