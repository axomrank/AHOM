All 850 image files and their attribution sidecars are retained. 25 unrelated Tripura photographs are quarantined, and 23 photographs have uncertain Kareng/Talatal identities requiring review. Categories and view types use source metadata rather than a complete visual audit.

Existing originals and downloaded reference-size renditions are preserved. Full-resolution source URLs and licence details remain in the sidecars and current manifest. Old download records under `00_catalogue/download_records/` retain historical paths and are not the current index.

Do not use later surviving masonry as direct evidence for a 15th-century town. Keep generated reconstructions outside this real-photograph library. See the guide for period and reference-selection limits.
