# Royal residential settlement — five character camera views

Created with the built-in image generation tool on 26 October 2026. Each shot uses the existing royal residential settlement as an architectural reference and a project character sheet for face, age, clothing, ornaments and headwear continuity. The camera is close enough for readable faces and textile detail, with houses and site features visible as context.

1. [Momai Tamuli — residential gate](01-momai-tamuli-residential-gate.png): eye-level medium portrait framed by the timber gateway, with the lane and raised homes behind.
2. [Numali Rajmao — house interior](02-numali-rajmao-house-interior.png): seated, three-quarter view in a timber-and-woven-wall domestic room, lit by an open wooden shutter.
3. [Sarbeswari — upper veranda](03-sarbeswari-upper-veranda.png): medium portrait with head ornaments and jewellery, looking over the pond and residences.
4. [Atan Burhagohain — lower veranda](04-atan-lower-veranda.png): slightly low viewpoint among posts, stairs and household baskets, preserving the brown drape and ivory garments.
5. [Ambika — smaller residence](05-ambika-smaller-residence.png): diagonal courtyard portrait with a smaller timber-and-thatch house and a domestic work shelter.

The user's “Rajmau” is represented using the local Numali Rajmao design; “Atan Borgohain” uses the available Atan Burhagohain design. These names identify the project's reconstructed visual characters. The characters include court officials as well as royal women, not a claim that every subject belongs to one royal family.

These are deliberate imagined character studies in the established 15th-century visual setting, not authenticated portraits or a claim that these historical individuals lived there together or in that century. Architecture, household arrangements, pose, face interpretation and costume backdating remain artistic. Comparative architectural reference: `../royal-houses-and-settlement/ahom-royal-houses-settlement-c1450.png`. The five character-sheet copies are preserved in `reference/`.

All existing guard uniforms remain dark navy with white headwraps and waist cloths. Hindu temple forms are not used as domestic house designs. All views are original generated files at the resolution recorded in [manifest.json](manifest.json), at least 1280×720; no artificial upscale was used. The complete prompts are in [prompts.json](prompts.json).
