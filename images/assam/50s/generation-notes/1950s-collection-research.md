# 1950s Assam collection — research and generation notes

Created 25 October 2026. Built-in image_gen workflow.

## Scope and interpretation

Eight scenes and eight reusable object assets, following the user's approved restrained 3D Namghar/Guru Asan style. The scenes imagine a modest rural Assamese-speaking Brahmaputra-valley setting around 1952. They are artistic reconstructions, not authenticated photographs of a particular village or evidence that all Assamese communities lived identically. Thatched buildings were selected for this collection; metal roofs and other building types also existed in the period. No claim is made that every later photograph proves an exact 1950s arrangement.

The existing minimal Guru Asan and Namghar interior are continuity references. Their earlier generated forms are user-approved visual designs, not independently authenticated period artifacts.

## Sources consulted and what each establishes

1. [INTACH — Assam State Museum kitchen model, ASMSM_011](https://icharchive.intach.org/Detail/objects/18664). Actual museum model provides floor-level hearth arrangement, cooking vessels, bamboo shelves, earthen wall surfaces and roof framework. Saved photograph: references/kitchen-assam-state-museum.jpg. Use is visual research; the generated scene is not a reproduction of the museum photograph or label.
2. [IGNCA — Textiles of Assam](https://ignca.gov.in/divisionss/janapada-sampada/northeastern-regional-centre/textiles-of-assam/). Describes domestic weaving, Assamese looms, throw and fly shuttle types, gamosa and other household cloth. Does not establish the precise date of our scene.
3. [North East Network — Saneki Weaves](https://northeastnetwork.org/saneki-weaves-nenterprise/). Working loom photograph supplies the frame, warp, heddles and beater arrangement. Saved photograph: references/handloom-nen.jpg. Contemporary construction reference, not a 1950s photograph.
4. [D'source / IIT Guwahati — Jakhoi and Khaloi](https://www.dsource.in/gallery/jakhoi-and-khaloi). Identifies the triangular bamboo fishing scoop and separate fish-holding creel, their materials and use. Saved three photographs: references/jakhoi-dsource.jpg, khaloi-dsource.jpg, jakhoi-khaloi-dsource.jpg. Khaloi is a container, not itself a fish trap; filenames describe the pair as a fishing set.
5. [PARI — In Darrang: ploughing a lonely furrow](https://ruralindiaonline.org/article/in-darrang-ploughing-a-lonely-furrow). Local craftsman's toolmaking and labelled construction sketch guide nangol beam, shoe, handle, iron share and juwal yoke. Saved construction sketch: references/plough-pari-02b.jpg. Additional workshop photos document wood and workmanship; they do not date the scene.
6. [Assam State Portal — Culture of Assam](https://usp2.amtron.in/about-us/391). Distinguishes practical plain farmer jaapi from ornate examples; describes bamboo/cane and tokou leaf construction, xorai, bota, brass and bell-metal crafts. Research access was partial; search excerpt available, page download failed.
7. [National Crafts Museum — Assamese Xorai, accession 05/141/D/NHHM(4)](https://nationalcraftsmuseum.nic.in/artifacts-detail/31686). Museum record/image search established the footed offering-vessel form. Full page and image retrieval timed out, so this was corroborating research, not an attached generation reference.
8. [The Copo — small Xorai](https://thecopo.in/products/xorai-small). Artisan product photograph supplied the actual silhouette used in generation: shallow covered tray, stem and flared foot. Saved: references/xorai-artisan.jpg. No prices or modern product claims used.
9. [MugaSilk — Kahi Bati / Sarthebari utensils](https://www.mugasilk.in/product-category/home-decor/assamese-bell-metal/kahi-bati/). Visual metalwork reference; generated objects are simplified domestic plate, bowls and ladle. Saved: references/utensils-sarthebari.jpg.
10. [Inditales — Majuli river island](https://inditales.com/river-island-of-majuli/). Photograph supplies long country-boat hull, raised ends and central woven canopy. Saved: references/boat-majuli.jpg. Remove source watermark, contemporary dress and people; do not copy photographic composition.
11. [Assam Inland Water Transport — history](https://www.asiwt.in/about). Establishes the department's 1958 origin and transition from wooden country boats to power vessels. Supports choosing a hand-powered village boat, without claiming engines were absent everywhere in the 1950s.
12. [XOBDO — Dheki](https://xobdo.org/dic/Dheki). Assamese dictionary photograph supplies the horizontal lever, short foot tail, axle supports and downward pestle/mortar relationship. Saved: references/dheki-xobdo.jpg. Modern metal wall and plastic excluded.
13. [Vernacular Architecture of Assam — typical homestead](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/essay/vernacular-architecture-of-assam/d/doc1085380.html). Academic study reproduced online describes ground-level thatched pajaghar buildings, earthen plinths, functional units around a courtyard and fenced compound. Used for layout, not exact building replication.
14. [Traditional climate-adaptive Assam-type houses — research paper](https://www.hrpub.org/download/20250330/CEA48-14840293.pdf). Corroborates wood/bamboo/reed construction, earthen infill, raised plinths and separate kitchen. Later construction examples are not copied as 1950s documentary evidence.
15. [Kipepeo — Flavours of Assam](https://www.kipepeo.in/trips/flavours-of-assam/). Modern field photograph guides village-market produce, ground-level selling and aisle arrangement only. Saved: references/market-kipepeo.jpg. Plastic and tarpaulin excluded from reconstruction.
16. [Remembering Shared Honor — Market Day in Assam, 1944](https://www.rshonor.net/node/14605). Archival search record consulted; direct image access returned HTTP 406, so its photograph was NOT used as a visual input and it is not counted as an inspected image reference.

## Decisions for the generated collection

- Plain field jaapi, not the brightly ornamented ceremonial jaapi.
- Assamese mekhela and sador treated as separate garments; everyday cotton dress, restrained borders.
- Dheki has a mechanically connected long beam, axle, short tail, downward pestle and mortar.
- Loom is hand-operated with warp, heddles, reed/beater and rollers; no industrial machine.
- Plough is a wooden ard with a small iron share and a yoke for two bullocks.
- Fishing asset is explicitly jakhoi plus khaloi; no unsupported mixture of generic traps.
- Brass and bell metal are distinguished in description; a metal-like gold colour alone does not identify the alloy.
- Village boat is hand-powered and follows the inspected country-boat silhouette.
- Modern objects are omitted to suit this particular period scene; their omission is not a universal historical claim.
- Generated details remain interpretive, especially working mechanisms, textile motifs and exact construction joinery. These PNGs are visual assets, not measured conservation drawings.
