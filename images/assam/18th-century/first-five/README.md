They do not authenticate the 18th-century costume, house type or activity. The separate Kachari and Bhuyan collections were not used.

Some monument weathering and exposed brick remain in the outputs despite prompts for maintained finishes. Read these as reference-guided concepts rather than exact restorations. The palace chamber use is proposed, not an authenticated room-function identification.

Household grain processing and basket work are artistic depictions, not technical demonstrations. Original generation files remain preserved at their paths in the manifest.
