# 18th-century Assam — Ahom monuments and everyday life

Created: 26 October 2026. Status: first five images generated; expansion scenes remain planned.

This collection follows the existing 15th-century and 1990s scene sets. Its focus is the period of the surviving masonry Rang Ghar and Garhgaon Kareng Ghar, together with royal, civic and ordinary household life.

**Date correction:** the requested monument-building period is the **18th century (1700s)**, rather than the 17th century (1600s). Rang Ghar's brick building dates to the 1740s; Garhgaon's surviving Kareng Ghar dates to 1752. Earlier timber buildings must be treated as separate phases.

Start with [the scene and production plan](SCENE_PLAN.md). Use circa 1755–1765 as a proposed shared setting for the first completed-monument scenes, subject to checking each building phase. Construction scenes have their own earlier dates.

Generated interpretations belong here; real photographs remain in [the historical reference library](../../referance/ahom/IMAGE_CREATION_GUIDE.md). Preserve original generation bundles under `output/imagegen/`.
