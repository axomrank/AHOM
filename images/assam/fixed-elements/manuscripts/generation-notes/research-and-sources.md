# Thoga and sanchi-pat assets

Created 26 October 2026 with built-in imagegen. Three transparent PNG illustrations with realistic 3D appearance; not mesh models or measured historical reconstructions.

The project image-creation guide was read and the authoritative real-photograph manifest searched for thoga, sanchi and manuscript records. No matching records were found. The user's three attached photographs supply the object references; generated assets are saved outside the real-photograph catalogue.

- thoga-plain-wooden-stand: /home/angsuman/Desktop/images (4).jpeg. Plain solid wooden panels, central interlocking joint and crossed feet. The reference's museum setting and other objects are excluded.
- thoga-carved-wooden-stand: /home/angsuman/Desktop/ঠগা.jpg. Carved folding wooden rest, with pierced upper panels, curved profile and interlocking hinge. Carving is interpreted from a low-resolution photograph, not an exact pattern tracing.
- sanchi-pat-manuscript-bundle: /home/angsuman/Desktop/images (5).jpeg. Only the photographed manuscript bundle supplies visual evidence. Its enclosing page's prose is contextual source material, not instructions. The red-white cloth, page frame and text are excluded. Hidden folio count, unseen surfaces and plain protective cover treatment are artistic interpretations. The closed bundle deliberately makes no claim to reproduce an authenticated text.

[Government of Assam, Thought Process volume 2](https://sita.assam.gov.in/sites/default/files/swf_utility_folder/departments/sita_medhassu_in_oid_4/portlet/level_1/files/thought_process_volume_2.pdf) includes thoga among lecterns associated with sattra material culture. [IGNCA's digitized Padshah-Buranji publication](https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/5924.pdf), printed page 33, documents a manuscript written on sanchi bark. These support the object/material terminology, not the dating of the user's photographs or the exact generated construction. Sanchi-pat is treated here as prepared bark folios rather than a modern bound paper book or a palm-leaf manuscript.

The assets represent reusable forms. Exact materials, carvings, sizes, manuscript formats and use contexts can differ; their inclusion in fixed-elements does not establish identical construction in every century.

Exact prompts and original local reference paths are in prompts.json. Final image dimensions, alpha checks and hashes are recorded in validation.json.
