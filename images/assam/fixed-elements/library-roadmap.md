# Assam reusable reference library — next elements

The library preserves recognisable traditional object forms and coherent natural assets. It does not assert that all objects, motifs, clothing, communities or landscapes were identical over hundreds of years.

## Existing assets

Guru Asan; dheki; wooden handloom; plain haluwa jaapi; xorai; kahi/bati utensils; jakhoi and khaloi; nangol and juwal; wooden country boat; Assamese Bihu dhol, pepa and toka. Nasoni and dhulia character sheets are stored in characters/.

## Natural assets and remaining possibilities

- Jati and bhaluka bamboo as separately referenced clumps, with mature culms and leaves visible.
- Tamul/areca palms, banana plants, paan vine with support, nahor tree and kopou orchid attached naturally to a host.
- Hollong for the appropriate forest habitat, rather than adding it indiscriminately to every village courtyard.
- Wetland reeds, beel margins, river sandbars and riparian vegetation as adaptable terrain components.
- Rice at seedling, transplanting, mature green and harvested stages as seasonal agricultural assets.

Natural landscape is reusable as an ecological vocabulary, not a fixed map. River channels, erosion, wetlands, forests, crops and settlement boundaries change over time.

## Reference sources for planning

- [ICFRE bamboo/rattan report](https://english.icfre.gov.in/pdf/UserFiles/File/status-report/part2.pdf) documents kula, chalani, khorahi, duli, doon and mats. It supports the tool list, not identical designs in every district or century.
- [Assam Forest Department — biodiversity](https://environmentandforest.assam.gov.in/information-services/biodiversity-of-assam-0) documents regional forest composition and commonly cultivated bamboo; use habitat-specific references.
- [Assam State Biodiversity Board booklet](https://asbb.gov.in/pdf/publication/booklets/Land-of-Blue-Hills-Red-Rivers.pdf) provides state-symbol identifications, including kopou and hollong.
- [IIT Guwahati / D'source — Gogona](https://www.dsource.in/resource/popular-folk-musical-instruments/making-0/gogona) provides making details for the bamboo instrument.
- [AGMC — handloom and handicraft products](https://agmcltd.assam.gov.in/portlets/handloom-handicraft-products) distinguishes garment and material product categories.
- Existing 1950s source research is in ../50s/generation-notes/1950s-collection-research.md.

## Asset standard

One main object/species per file, clear whole-object view, source record, dimensions or scale cue when available, neutral light, consistent realistic materials, and transparent alpha for isolated reusable objects. Characters get stable multi-view identities and separately researched costume variants. Every record should distinguish enduring form from period-specific implementation.
