# Assam reusable elements: next pack

Created 25 October 2026. Thirty transparent PNG illustrations with a restrained realistic 3D appearance: six household objects, six textiles, four instruments, four vessels, seven plants and three accessory files. These are raster assets, not mesh models or measured technical drawings. Exact generation prompts and per-asset evidence are recorded in generation-jobs.json; original generated-file paths and the cymbal correction are recorded in file-provenance.json.

The research combined inspected local photographs, museum material, the 1961 handicrafts census, botanical photographs and contemporary artisan examples. These document forms and materials; they do not establish that every decoration, dimension, garment arrangement or landscape was unchanged across centuries. Contemporary commercial examples are identified below. No dated 1950s provenance is asserted for this reusable pack.

## Household objects

- Kula: [British Museum, Jorhat miniature collected in 1893](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/A_As1893-0612-16). A miniature establishes the silhouette and woven construction; working-size proportions are reconstructed.
- Chaloni: [artisan bamboo sieve photograph](https://www.mystore.in/en/product/bamboo-chalni).
- Khorahi: [named basket photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_bamboo_Basket_(_khorahi).jpg), Jyoti Chiring, 31 March 2025, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Generated empty, omitting the photographed contents and surroundings.
- Duli: [paddy-seed storage basket photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%22Duli%22_-_a_large_bamboo_basket_used_for_storing_seeds_of_paddy,_mustard,_etc.,_commonly_used_in_Assam_02.jpg), AjayDas, 4 January 2024, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Generated as an isolated interpretation.
- Doon: [1961 Census, Selected Handicrafts of Assam](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/31710/download/34891/52611_1961_SEL.pdf), PDF page 199, describes a conical bamboo grain measure with a supporting bottom ring. This asset is reconstructed from text; the khorahi photograph supplies material guidance, not proof of the doon shape. Basket capacities and local forms vary.
- Pira: census terminology above and a [general low wooden seat reference](https://www.exoticindiaart.com/product/homeandliving/14-rectangular-wooden-puja-peeta-pedestal-chowki-ddp476/). The photograph is not a dated Assamese specimen; the plain board-and-two-support construction is an interpretation.

## Textiles

- [Assam State Museum textile catalogue](https://museums.assam.gov.in/sites/default/files/swf_utility_folder/departments/museum_medhassu_in_oid_5/portlet/level_2/costumes_of_the_ahom_royalty.pdf), inspected PDF pages 12, 14 and 17, supports distinguishing riha, tube-form mekhela, sador and gamosa. Garment construction and material references do not date the generated decorative patterns.
- Muga riha uses a [named pure-muga artisan photograph](https://www.xunalixuta.com/product/28451564/RIHA--PURE-MUGA--MR-D14J22). Its border is one example, not a universal riha pattern. Mekhela and sador are separate garment assets with restrained interpreted motifs.
- Eri texture: [Handa Textiles raw eri](https://www.handatextiles.com/fabrics/p/raw-eri-silk).
- Pat texture: [Korobi natural-white Assamese mulberry silk](https://korobidesign.com/products/natural-white-with-contrast-blouse-handloom-mulberry-silk-saree-from-assam), used only for fabric appearance. Both silk swatches are new isolated compositions.

## Instruments

- Gogona and xutuli: [named folk-instrument photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Assamese_folk_wind_instruments_Gogona_and_Xutuli.jpg), Nayan j Nath, 7 March 2024, [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The clay asset follows one photographed whistle variant. [D'source gogona construction](https://www.dsource.in/resource/popular-folk-musical-instruments/making-0/gogona) corroborates the instrument type.
- Taal: [Assamese artisan cymbal photograph](https://www.kahibati.com/tal.html) and [D'source Bihu taal](https://www.dsource.in/resource/popular-folk-musical-instruments/making-0/bihu-taal). A small Bihu-use pair is interpreted without a measured variant claim. The initial ambiguous overlap was regenerated into two clearly separate cymbals.
- Baahi: [Sahapedia Bihu photographs](https://www.sahapedia.org/bihu-photos). The flute is partly obscured in the source; the six-hole layout is reconstructed rather than calibrated for manufacture.

## Vessels

- Bota: [artisan offering tray](https://www.kahibati.com/bota-saras-88.html), simplified to plain metal.
- Lota/ghati: [Assamese wedding vessel set](https://www.kahibati.com/wedding-set-900gm.html). The vendor uses the name ghati; naming is not uniform across contexts.
- Tekeli: [Barpeta Hira and Kumar pottery study](https://www.academia.edu/110870820/Pottery_traditions_among_the_Hira_and_Kumar_potters_of_Barpeta_district_in_Assam), vessel E in the inspected comparative figure. Other vessels in that figure were excluded.
- Saki: [Ministry of Tourism Kati Bihu photograph](https://utsav.gov.in/view-event/kati-bihu-or-kangali-bihu). Isolated unlit clay lamp, omitting the photographed post and field.

## Plants

- Jati and bhaluka: identified culm photographs of [Bambusa tulda](https://www.guaduabamboo.com/blog/bambusa-tulda) and [Bambusa balcooa](https://www.guaduabamboo.com/blog/bambusa-balcooa). Full crowns are reconstructed. Regional species context: [Assam Forest Department](https://environmentandforest.assam.gov.in/information-services/biodiversity-of-assam-0). Common names can vary.
- Tamul: [NParks Areca catechu](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/florafaunaweb/flora/2/5/2587), photographs credited to NParks/Jessica Teo. Buildings and surrounding vegetation were omitted.
- Banana: [Assamese homestead banana photograph](https://asomiya.krishijagran.com/webstories/benefit-of-bhim-banana.html). Generated as a generic homestead plant; no certified cultivar identity is claimed.
- Paan: [betel-vine habit photograph](https://www.popmama.com/big-kid/10-12-years-old/ciri-ciri-tanaman-sirih-yang-merambat-00-cjzq3-84sl3q). Photographed outside Assam, used for climbing habit and heart-shaped leaves only.
- Nahor: [identified Mesua ferrea tree photograph](https://www.iplantz.com/plant/1047/mesua-ferrea/) and Assam forestry context above. This is an illustrative whole-tree asset, not a diagnostic botanical plate.
- Kopou: [first-hand Rhynchostylis retusa photographs from Similipal](https://satyeshnaik.blogspot.com/2014/08/orchids-from-similipal-hills.html), used for tiny spotted flowers, racemes, leaves and bark attachment. The location is outside Assam; the source does not document an Assamese landscape.

## Accessories

- Fulam jaapi follows only the decorated hat in the user's local nasoni photograph, bihu-dance-in-assam-assamese-girl-bihu-dress-with-japi-in-her-hand-2XR27GB.jpg. People, source background, stock marks and lettering were omitted. This decorated hat is distinct from a plain field hat.
- Kopou hair adornment combines the botanical source above with the local portrait's hair-placement concept. It is an artistic wearable sprig rather than a species identification of the portrait flowers.
- Jewellery uses contemporary artisan [junbiri](https://zangfai.in/assamese-traditional-junbiri-set-red-silver-golden) and [dholbiri](https://zangfai.in/assamese-traditional-dholbiri-setblack-beads-pure-silver-base-gold-dipped) examples. One PNG contains both distinct sets. Exact bead, enamel and pendant arrangements are example designs rather than universal historical patterns.

## Delivery and checks

All 30 final PNGs passed file integrity and nontrivial alpha checks. The composited overview was visually inspected. Final source-file hashes, image dimensions and transparency measurements are in validation.json. The archive contains generated assets, notes and a preview; downloaded source photographs and PDFs remain in the local research directory and are excluded from the archive.
