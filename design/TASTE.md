# Ahom visual taste

Synthesised from the reference set the user supplied: the Mula Gabharu, Sankardev, Godapani–Joymoti, Rudra Singha and Gadadhar Singha posters, the Ahom crest on brown, and the maroon "AHOM 1" card. The fonts are the Bengali-script display faces in the user's download folder (`.fonts-dl`).

## What the references share
- **Paper, not screen.** A warm cream ground with a faint grain. Ink is dark umber, not black.
- **Photos as torn prints.** Each picture is cut along a ragged lower edge, with a soft shadow, and sits off-centre on the page.
- **Botanical sprigs at the corners.** Neem leaves, bamboo, a feather, and a fine line sprig. They overlap the photo edge and never sit in the text.
- **A hand-lettered note.** A short script line top-right, underlined with a stroke.
- **Name and subtitle at bottom-left.** Classical serif capitals for the name, an italic serif line beneath.
- **The crest.** A gold beast on a brown ground, inside a scalloped cartouche with a dotted rim. Gold and maroon.

## The palette, in use
- Paper `#F3ECDD`, grain on top.
- Ink (text) `#3D2B1F`, secondary text `#6B5544`.
- Lacquer maroon `#5A0E1C` for titles, primary actions and the seal bar.
- Gilt `#B8862B` for the cartouche, the Roman numerals and the sparkles. Not for body text.
- Sage `#5E7A45` for botanical sprigs only.
- Earth brown `#6B3E1F` is reserved for the crest's ground.

## Type
- **Assamese, reading:** Tiro Bangla. Its manuscript-like hand is the closest to the chronicle.
- **Assamese, the one large word:** Galada. Brushed and calligraphic, it matches the poster hand.
- **Latin names and titles:** Marcellus, in capitals with tracking, as on the posters.
- **Subtitles:** Alegreya italic.
- **Notes:** Dancing Script.
- Atma, Baloo Da 2, Hind Siliguri and Mina were considered. They read as modern signage, so they are not used.

## Composition rules
1. A photo is always a torn print, never a rectangle with a border.
2. A sprig sits on a corner and overlaps a photo edge. It stays out of the text column.
3. The Assamese word is always set large in a gilt cartouche.
4. Names go bottom-left in capitals, with the italic line beneath.
5. Script notes go top-right, with one underline stroke, and are short.

## Open
- The script note copy is new and needs the user's approval.
- An Assamese reader should check every Assamese word before anything ships.
- The Tai Ahom script is not used. It would need a reader who knows the spelling.
