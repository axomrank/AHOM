# Round 1 concept capsules (withheld until the human reacts)

Shared working copy (provisional, from the site's own text): "Six hundred years on the Brahmaputra";
"In 1228 a Tai prince named Sukaphaa crossed the Patkai hills into Assam…"; "Forty-one reigns";
dates 1228–1838 (Latin and Assamese digits); Assamese words আহোম, স্বৰ্গদেউ, বুৰঞ্জী.

Fonts (all open-source, not Google): Lohit Assamese (SIL OFL 1.1, Assamese script); Gentium Plus
(SIL OFL 1.1 with Reserved Font Name, Latin with diacritics); IBM Plex Serif / Sans / Sans Condensed /
Mono (SIL OFL 1.1 with Reserved Font Name). Licence texts are in `fonts/`. No paid font is used.

## A: Pothi (one letterform colour)
- Premise: a palm-leaf manuscript is long and narrow; text runs in short horizontal lines with a wide blank band on the left where the binding string passes.
- Type: Lohit Assamese for the Assamese lines; Gentium Plus Regular for English. Two families, one size step for Assamese.
- Case: sentence case. Scale: Assamese 2.2rem, title 1.4rem, body 1.1rem.
- Colour: ground #EFE5CF, ink #2A1B12. One colour.
- Space: text column about 30ch, left offset 16vw, generous top and bottom padding.
- Signature: a narrow leaf of text left-shifted beside an empty binding margin.
- Invariant: narrow column, left binding space, Assamese lines stacked above the title.
- Risk: may read as an empty page at thumbnail size.

## B: Reign line (two colours)
- Premise: 41 reigns on one 610-year line; distance is time.
- Type: IBM Plex Sans Condensed (SemiBold, Regular) and IBM Plex Mono for the caps label.
- Case: uppercase tracked labels; 1228 and 1838 pinned to opposite edges across the full width.
- Colour: ground #F4F3EE; primary ink #1E2328; secondary ochre-red #9B3B22 for the labels and the 1838 date.
- Space: full-width date line, lede in a column at the right, Assamese word at left.
- Signature: the two dates as the ends of a line, spaced by the page width itself.
- Risk: the wide date line depends on viewport width.

## C: Gamosa weave (two colours)
- Premise: the gamosa is woven red and dark; warp and weft alternate.
- Type: IBM Plex Serif SemiBold for the justified block; Lohit Assamese for Assamese words.
- Case: sentence case, justified text with word spacing set to 0.12em.
- Colour: ground #FBF8F1; red #A11B22 and black #1B1716 alternating word by word.
- Space: one justified block filling the page with a small caption.
- Signature: alternating colour per word forms a woven texture made only of language.
- Risk: colour alternation may read as highlighting rather than weave; the human must judge.

## D: Bihu call and response (one colour)
- Premise: a Bihu singer calls a line and the reply answers it from the other side.
- Type: Gentium Plus Italic for the call lines, small caps for refrains, Lohit Assamese for the Assamese refrains.
- Case: sentence case with small-cap refrains.
- Colour: ground #1A2A22; text #F0E6CC. One colour.
- Space: lines alternate between left and right indents; gaps between stanzas.
- Signature: alternating indents set as call and reply.
- Risk: the layout may read as a poem, which is fine only if the typography carries it.

## E: The river (two colours)
- Premise: the Brahmaputra bends; the lines wander to the right and back.
- Type: IBM Plex Sans Light for the title; IBM Plex Serif Italic for the lede; Lohit Assamese for the closing line.
- Case: sentence case; large Light title.
- Colour: ground #E3ECEC; slate #1D3A46 primary; ochre #B5772A for the date and closing line.
- Space: lede lines indented by varying amounts in a meandering path.
- Signature: the lede's indentation follows a river bend.
- Risk: meander may look like a mistake if the indents are too irregular.

## F: The mounds (three colours)
- Premise: the royal burial mounds rise from the ground; text stacks upward from the base.
- Type: IBM Plex Serif Bold caps for the title; Regular for the lede; IBM Plex Mono for small labels; Lohit Assamese.
- Case: uppercase title, sentence case lede.
- Colour: ground #2A2319 (earth); bone #E5D6B4 primary; #A8926B secondary; #7C6A4E tertiary.
- Space: content anchored to the bottom edge, decreasing in size upward.
- Signature: the stack of text as a mound, widest at the base.
- Risk: the dark earth ground may be heavy.

## G: Satra litany (two colours)
- Premise: a hymn read in repetition, centred and stacked.
- Type: Gentium Plus Regular and Bold for the English; Lohit Assamese at large size.
- Case: sentence case; the bold label is spaced caps.
- Colour: ground #FBF9F4; indigo #22305A for Assamese; brown #7A5A22 for English.
- Space: centred column, lines repeated with non-semantic repetition of the title.
- Signature: the Assamese lines in indigo and English lines in brown, as a litany of two voices.
- Risk: repetition of the title may read as padding.

## H: Sanctum silence (one colour)
- Premise: the king's inner sanctum is silent; the largest thing in the room is the name of the office, the rest is barely there.
- Type: Lohit Assamese at about 30vw for the single word; IBM Plex Sans Light small text.
- Case: sentence case; tracked caps for the date.
- Colour: ground #0C0D0F; text #E8E2D2. One colour.
- Space: a single word cropped at the bottom edge, three small text blocks at the corners.
- Signature: a huge word cut at the base against almost no text.
- Risk: the cropped word may be read as a clipping error; the human must judge.

## I: Polyglot (four colours)
- Premise: the Ahom court spoke and wrote in several languages; each has its own role.
- Type: Lohit Assamese for Assamese; Gentium Plus Regular and Italic for English; IBM Plex Mono Medium for numerals; the name "Sukaphaa" in Gentium Plus Italic.
- Case: sentence case; the name is italic.
- Colour: ground #F5F0E4; maroon #6A1424 primary (Assamese words); ink #2B2B2B secondary (English); ochre #B07A22 tertiary (Assamese digits); green #2F6B5A accent (the name).
- Space: twelve-column grid; Assamese left, English right, numerals beside.
- Signature: each language keeps its own colour and column.
- Risk: four colours can read as decoration; each must carry real text, which it does.

## J: Mud of the field (three colours)
- Premise: the battles were fought in wet paddy fields; the text is heavy and pressed down.
- Type: IBM Plex Sans Condensed Bold uppercase for the title; Regular for the lede; Lohit Assamese.
- Case: uppercase title with tight tracking; sentence case lede.
- Colour: ground #3B2E22 (mud); cream #EFE3CB primary; #B9A27F secondary; red #C4452B accent for "Brahmaputra" and the dates.
- Space: title fills the width and the height; lede at top; small row at the base.
- Signature: a heavy condensed title sunk into the base of the page.
- Risk: very large type may overflow on narrow screens.
