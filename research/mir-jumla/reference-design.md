# MIR JUMLA — reference and design

Prepared 16 October 2026. Modern artistic reconstruction. See ../mughal-bengal-14-research.md for source access, provenance and limitations. Proposed faces, ages, garment cuts and colours are artistic choices except where explicitly connected to a source portrait description. No individual local scene is treated as an authenticated portrait.

Mir Muhammad Said, Mir Jumla II, tall Iranian-born Mughal governor in 1662 around age 71. Source Royal Collection RCIN 1005069.f c.1660 identifies famously tall courtier. Depict tall lean elderly man, light olive-brown skin, elongated narrow face, high prominent curved nose, hooded dark eyes, deeply lined forehead, long neatly kept silver-grey beard and moustache. Refined dignified serious expression. Pale warm grey silk-cotton ankle-to-midcalf Mughal jama, fine cream patka with subtle gold woven ends, narrow cream trousers, light cream folded turban with modest jewel clasp, a simple pale shawl over one shoulder, dark leather mojari, small sheathed court dagger. Avoid young muscular warrior, fantasy armor, crown or huge necklaces. Reconstruction informed by portrait record, not claim exact portrait copy.
