# Burmese and British — 12 completed character sheets

Completed 17 October 2026 using the built-in image_gen tool. All twelve sheets were inspected individually, saved to the project, given thumbnails and face crops, and linked in the master catalogue. This supersedes the generation-blocked status in the earlier ten-person dossier.

Each landscape sheet contains four full-body views (front, left profile, back, three-quarter) and four bust views (face close-up, right profile, low angle, looking down). The images are modern artistic reconstructions, not authenticated historical likenesses. Facial designs, exact costumes, unseen sides and some apparent ages remain interpretive.

Scene-level catalogue associations do not prove which individual occupies a position. All assignments above are production choices. No authenticated portrait was established for the less-documented commanders in this task. The British Museum dress manuscript is later nineteenth-century comparative evidence, not proof of an 1810s or 1820s individual's outfit. Existing historical summaries were preserved; this batch adds artwork and provenance.

## Sources checked

- [Government of Assam, Jorhat history](https://jorhat.assam.gov.in/about-us/history-jorhat): Welsh expedition context, Bandula at Mahgarh, Richards's campaign, Scott's commission, Campbell and the treaty, restoration and annexation. The timeline contains conflicting dates elsewhere and is not treated as an infallible chronology.
- [National Portrait Gallery, John Smart's Cornwallis, 1792, NPG 4316](https://www.npg.org.uk/collections/search/portrait/mw01484/Charles-Cornwallis-1st-Marquess-Cornwallis): sitter identity and dated portrait context.
- [Nelson-Atkins, Smart's Cornwallis miniature](https://www.nelson-atkins.org/starr/contents/volume-4/John-Smart/India/F65-41-33/): contemporary portrait and dress discussion.
- [British Museum, parabaik 2011,3015.1](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/A_2011-3015-1): comparative Burmese court regalia, court garments and military dress. Search-index text was accessible; direct page retrieval failed on this run. Later evidence, used cautiously.
- [British Museum, Myanmar timeline](https://www.britishmuseum.org/exhibitions/burma-myanmar/myanmars-many-cultures-timeline): Bodawpaya and regional historical context.
- [National Army Museum, First Burma War](https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/first-burma-war): Campbell's campaign and Bandula's role/death. Accounts differ on the projectile responsible; no such detail is depicted or newly asserted in the catalogue.
- [IGNCA-hosted historical text, printed page 107](https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/42192.pdf): Jenkins's appointment, birth and service, and succession to Robertson. Indexed text was accessible; direct PDF retrieval failed on this run.
- [Dictionary of Indian Biography scan](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f9/Dictionary_of_Indian_biography%3B_%28IA_dictionaryofindi00buck%29.pdf): Robertson's civilian career. This is an older reference work, not contemporary portrait evidence; indexed OCR includes adjacent entries and must be read cautiously.
- [Earlier ten-person research dossier](burmese-british-10-research.md): prior source checks, prepared prompts and limitations for Kiamingi, Tilwa, the two kings and the original British roster.
