# AURANGZEB — reference and design

Prepared 16 October 2026. Modern artistic reconstruction. See ../mughal-bengal-14-research.md for source access, provenance and limitations. Proposed faces, ages, garment cuts and colours are artistic choices except where explicitly connected to a source portrait description. No individual local scene is treated as an authenticated portrait.

Emperor Aurangzeb Alamgir at about age 49 around 1667, NOT elderly ninety-year-old portrait. Historical Mughal portrait tradition: slender physique, light warm olive-brown skin, long narrow face, prominent aquiline nose, dark slightly hooded eyes, neatly trimmed dark beard with early grey strands and moustache, serious reserved expression. White fine muslin long Mughal jama over white narrow trousers, understated pale gold floral woven patka, low white folded imperial turban with a small emerald-and-pearl sarpech and restrained plume, one restrained strand of pearls, fine gold-toned mojari. Small jeweled sheathed dagger at waist. No huge crown, armor, halo, prayer book action or nineteenth-century royal costume. Reconstructed three-dimensional man inspired by portrait conventions, not exact authenticated face.
