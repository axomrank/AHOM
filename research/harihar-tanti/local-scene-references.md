# HARIHAR TANTI — design record

Prepared 16 October 2026. Modern artistic reconstruction; no authenticated personal likeness established in the consulted material. Face, apparent age, particular garment cuts, headwraps, footwear and colours are artistic decisions, not documented personal attributes.

Later Moamoria organiser associated with the north bank Brahmaputra, late 1780s Assam. Distinct man about 42, wiry compact build, warm deep-brown skin, narrow oblong face, alert close-set dark eyes, slightly hooked broad nose, prominent ears, receding hairline, short black hair in small low knot, thin moustache with clean-shaven chin. Practical undyed coarse handwoven cotton short collarless upper garment with elbow sleeves, off-white calf-length dhoti, faded indigo waistcloth, narrow red-bordered off-white shoulder cloth over one shoulder, simple off-white tied headcloth and bare feet. Modest field organiser, no royal regalia or jewellery, no loom props derived just from surname, no weapons. Thoughtful firm expression.
