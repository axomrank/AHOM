# RAMAKANTA — design record

Prepared 16 October 2026. Modern artistic reconstruction; no authenticated personal likeness established in the consulted material. Face, apparent age, particular garment cuts, headwraps, footwear and colours are artistic decisions, not documented personal attributes.

Son of Naharkhora Saikia, installed as rebel king in 1769. New young adult male about 28, medium-brown skin, tall slender frame, long oval face, high cheekbones, dark almond eyes, straight nose, black hair neatly tied at nape, fine black moustache, no beard. Subtle family resemblance to an angular-faced father without copying any source figure. Modest early accession court costume: natural ivory cotton-silk knee-length wrap-front tunic with narrow muted gold edging, white dhoti, deep maroon woven waist sash, cream shoulder cloth with restrained red geometric borders, low cream folded cloth headwrap with narrow gold woven edge, plain brown leather slippers. No jeweled crown, throne, necklace or weapons. He is neither Ragha nor the captive Lakshmi Singha. Specific clothes and age are artistic choices.
