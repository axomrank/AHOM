# Five Moamoria character sheets

Prepared 16 October 2026. Completes the five character sheets requested after Ragha Moran. Supersedes the earlier dossier's first-sheet-only production scope. Eight-view photorealistic sheets are modern artistic reconstructions, not authenticated portraits. Serious expressions throughout.

## Evidence and limits

- Naharkhora: first-revolt leader and father of the installed king. Older apparent age is a design inference. Local outbreak scene links him at scene level without assigning an individual figure; its textiles inform the new design only.
- Ramakanta: retain catalogue name; university material calls the son Ramananda. Young adult age and subtle father-son resemblance are invented design choices. Do not identify the crowned captive in the local imprisonment scene as him: that captive is Lakshmi Singha.
- Harihar: later organiser and north-bank leader. Baruah equates Hawha with Harihar and includes Majuli; university material distinguishes Harihar on the north bank and Howha on Majuli. Preserve disagreement; no new alias is imposed on the catalogue. Plain practical clothing is an artistic brief, not a documented personal costume.
- Bharat: later ruler at Rangpur, distinct from first-phase Ramakanta. Coinage supports rulership, not facial features or an exact crown. Catalogue chronology variants remain unchanged.
- Sarbananda: Bengmara ruler and builder, separate from Bharat's centre. Official district account supports 1791 capital transfer. No exact headgear, portrait or ancestry is inferred from this evidence.
