# Konseng — two attire sheets

User requested Naga attire and Borpatragohain attire, keeping one character identity.

Local references:
- `media/scenes/ahom/1497-1603-imperial-expansion/02_Konseng_Enters_the_Ahom_Court.webp`: standing foreground man at left, primary woven clothing, hair and profile.
- `media/scenes/ahom/1497-1603-imperial-expansion/03_Konseng_Appointed_First_Barpatra_Gohain.webp`: kneeling man at left, supporting costume and profile.
- `media/characters/thaomung-burhagohain/turnaround-1.webp`: simplified cream official dress, used for minister attire only.

Also inspected `07_Konseng_and_the_Western_Frontier.webp`; battle regalia not carried over to the simple court-attire sheet.

The local scenes show a partial face. Completing it and keeping the same adult age across both costumes are artistic design decisions for visual continuity. The Naga attire reproduces this project's selected scene costume and does not claim to represent all Naga communities or a verified historical ensemble. Existing chronology differences in catalogue metadata are not resolved by these illustrations. No external research used. Built-in image generation.
