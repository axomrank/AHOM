# ABU BAKR — reference and design

Prepared 16 October 2026. Modern artistic reconstruction. See ../mughal-bengal-14-research.md for source access, provenance and limitations. Proposed faces, ages, garment cuts and colours are artistic choices except where explicitly connected to a source portrait description. No individual local scene is treated as an authenticated portrait.

Mughal expedition commander in Assam 1615, Jahangir-era officer, not the seventh-century caliph. Interpretive man about 38, tall lean athletic build, olive-brown skin, long angular face, pronounced straight nose, grey-brown eyes, neat black pointed full beard and moustache. Muted teal-green knee-length Mughal wrap jama under short practical iron mail vest, sand trousers, plum woven patka, low cream turban without royal jewels, brown leather riding shoes and sheathed sword. Serious professional officer with realistic early seventeenth-century material.
