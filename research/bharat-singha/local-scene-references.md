# BHARAT SINGHA — design record

Prepared 16 October 2026. Modern artistic reconstruction; no authenticated personal likeness established in the consulted material. Face, apparent age, particular garment cuts, headwraps, footwear and colours are artistic decisions, not documented personal attributes.

Later Moamoria rebel ruler at Rangpur in the 1790s, distinct from Ramakanta and Sarbananda. Assamese man about 40, broad sturdy build, medium brown skin, broad square face with full cheeks, level dark eyes, broad nose, thick black moustache with clean-shaven chin, thick black hair tied neatly behind head. Restrained regional court dress: warm pale ochre cotton-silk knee-length wrap-front tunic, dark maroon narrow woven borders at neck cuffs and hem, ivory dhoti, muted red-gold woven waist sash, pale cream shoulder chadar with red woven ends, modest ochre folded headwrap with a narrow red band, plain brown leather slippers. No jeweled crown, ornamental armor, large necklaces, weapons or religious markings. Dignified serious expression. Exact face age and dress are provisional artistic interpretation, not evidence from coin portraits.
