# Early British resistance: two final scenes

Prepared 17 October 2026. Scope: AHOM-276 and AHOM-277. No dedicated character sheets or authenticated likenesses were found for Gomdhar Konwar, Piyali Phukan, Jiuram, Dhananjoy or Rutherford. Figures, clothing and staging are therefore explicitly provisional; existing royal faces are not repurposed.

## Sources checked

- [Gomdhar Konwar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gomdhar_Konwar): 1828 resistance and attempted restoration. Secondary orientation; no visual evidence for the exact assembly, costume or ritual.
- [British rule in Assam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_rule_in_Assam): distinguishes the 1828 and 1830 resistance from the later 1857–58 episode. Some claims are marked as needing citations, so do not use it alone to settle exact details.
- [ETV Bharat, “Martyrs of freedom struggle from Assam await recognition”](https://www.etvbharat.com/english/state/assam/martyrs-of-freedom-struggle-from-assam-awaits-recognition/na20200925181218073): contemporary commemorative reporting for Piyali and Jiuram; the catalogue retains uncertainty over day and site.
- [Government of India, Mann Ki Baat, July 2024, p.31](https://cbcindia.gov.in/wp-content/themes/boc/mann_ki_baat/e_july24/files/basic-html/page31.html): search-indexed public-history acknowledgement of Gomdhar's 1828 resistance and Piyoli/Jiuram in 1830.
- [North Bengal University repository, historical chapter](https://ir.nbu.ac.in/bitstreams/3a3623c9-d775-4560-8c8d-652814ddb22f/download): search-indexed text places Piyali and Jiuram's hanging in August 1830. Date and spelling differences remain unresolved.

## Depiction decisions

276 selects a rural mobilisation supporting Gomdhar near Jorhat, before the unsuccessful march. A partial profile and three-quarter back view avoid presenting an invented face as a known portrait. No precise crowning ritual, British officer identity, battlefield layout or victory is asserted. The unnamed elder is not individually labelled Dhananjoy.

277 shows the solemn moments before execution. Both men are alive; the empty gallows supplies context without showing suspension or bodily harm. No exact site monument or day is depicted. Their clothing, guard uniforms, official and witnesses are reconstructions. Jiuram's Dulia Baruah/Medhi naming and August/September dating remain qualified.

Piyali Phukan of 1830 is not Piyali Barua of 1858. Maniram Dewan is absent. Badan Chandra is linked in the catalogue only as Piyali's father and is not physically present. No modern Indian flag, later pith helmet, authenticated regimental badge or invented readable proclamation.

## Production

Built-in image_gen; one independent painting per scene. Exact prompts and generated-original paths are saved per scene and in the final manifest. Review covers dignity, scene clarity, anatomy, period context and the absence of substituted known faces. Final WebP images, thumbnails and PNG masters are retained in the workspace after review.
