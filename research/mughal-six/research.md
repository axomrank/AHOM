## Scene decisions

244: Mangaldoi and aged Pratap in restrained wedding/alliance reception. Brass tray and flowers suggest ceremony, not a proven exact rite.
245: Pratap receives news of blocked passage, illustrating the cause of the Kachari war. No invented Jaintia princess portrait, later battle or wedding tableau.
246: Balinarayan receives asylum, with provisional distinct Koch noble appearance. No formal enthronement conflated with the first arrival.
247: Night attack on expedition boats, Abu Bakr reacts and Satrajit escapes separately. No graphic death or readable victory inscription.
248: Momai Tamuli directs river defence as one representative moment. Elderly king and opposing commanders are not invented as personally present.
249: Silver hand-striking workshop with royal oversight. Layout and visit illustrative; no fake readable coin legend, modern mint equipment or claim exact dies reproduced.

## Environment

Assam's wet Brahmaputra valley, reeds, bamboo/cane, broadleaf vegetation, paddy margins, alluvial mud/sandbars and humid haze. Timber, bamboo, thatch, woven walls, earthen ramparts and wooden palisades fit the selected settings. No later Rang Ghar, Talatal Ghar or brick Kareng Ghar. River craft, military deployments and workshops are conservative artistic staging, not measured archaeological plans. Period matchlocks and modest cannon allowed in seventeenth-century naval scenes; no modern weapons.
