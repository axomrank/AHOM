# NAHARKHORA SAIKIA — design record

Prepared 16 October 2026. Modern artistic reconstruction; no authenticated personal likeness established in the consulted material. Face, apparent age, particular garment cuts, headwraps, footwear and colours are artistic decisions, not documented personal attributes.

Moran co-leader of the first Moamoria revolt, Assam 1769, father of Ramakanta. Distinct mature man about 55, strong lean working build, medium-brown weathered skin, broad angular face, high cheekbones, deep-set dark eyes, broad straight nose, black hair streaked grey tied low, short salt-and-pepper moustache and short beard. Plain cream cotton collarless elbow-sleeve upper shirt, deep forest-green wrapped dhoti to lower calf, narrow muted red geometric woven cross-body cloth and matching waist sash, simple cream cloth headwrap, barefoot. Supplied scene is textile/context reference ONLY, neither foreground figure is a verified Naharkhora; create new older face, do not copy the young men. No weapon.
