# Final eleven regional rulers — character sheets

Completed 17 October 2026 with the built-in image_gen tool, after reviewing the project catalogue, local artwork and linked historical sources. Each sheet has four full-body views and four face/bust views, serious expressions and an ARTISTIC RECONSTRUCTION footer.

These are modern visual reconstructions. No authenticated individual likeness or exact personal outfit was established for these eleven. Apparent ages, faces, headgear, jewelry and unseen garment details are artistic choices. Regional motifs in modern project scenes are continuity references, not proof of historical clothing.

## Sources and limits

- [Binay Barman, History of Two Unexplored Capital Cities of Kamata-Kuchbehar Kingdom, Heritage 11.2](https://heritageuniversityofkerala.com/JournalPDF/Volume11.2/70.pdf): Kamata capital context; explicitly notes disagreement about the kingdom's emergence. Not costume evidence.
- [Sikkim University repository, Paresh Borah thesis](https://dspace.cus.ac.in/jspui/bitstream/1/4571/1/Paresh%20Borah.pdf): indexed discussion of Durlabhnarayan and literary patronage. Used as secondary historical context, not personal portrait evidence.
- [Chutia kingdom, cited epigraphic discussion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chutia_kingdom): secondary overview of Nandi, Satyanarayana, the proposed Ratnanarayana identification and the last rulers. Its references discuss Maheswar Neog (1977) and Jae-Eun Shin (2020); original copperplates and complete original scholarly editions were not independently inspected in this task. Facial appearance and precise costumes cannot be inferred from the cited genealogies.
- [Cooch Behar district, Royal History](https://coochbehar.gov.in/about-district/history/): official regional overview of Nara Narayan and the Koch succession/campaign context. No claim that it supplies an authenticated portrait for the sheets.
- [Banglapedia, Ghiyasuddin Azam Shah](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Ghiyasuddin_Azam_Shah): identifies the Ilyas Shahi ruler and Kamarupa expedition; cultural and administrative context. It gives 1389–1410, while other sources use 1390–1411; no exact reign dates were printed on the sheet or changed in the catalogue.
- [Government of India, 1961 Nowgong district census handbook](https://censusindia.gov.in/nada/index.php/catalog/29564/download/32745/21962_1961_NOW.pdf): indexed historical discussion of Tamradhwaj, Maibong and Rudra Singha's Jaintia campaign. Context only, not dress evidence.
- [IGNCA-hosted Introduction to Assam](https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/34806.pdf): indexed historical discussion of the Kachari and Jaintia rulers and Ahom audience. Exact visitor identities in the local modern scene remain unverified.
- [Imphal East district, Places of Interest](https://imphaleast.nic.in/places-of-interest/): Bhagyachandra/Govindaji tradition and regional context.
- [Manipur government tourism document](https://manipur.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/eoi_tourism.pdf): identifies Bhagyachandra's association with Raas Leela. It does not establish the king's exact costume.

Source searches and source text were used for identity, period and role checks. No remote image was passed to generation: all eleven used local project images. Epigraphic genealogy, cultural traditions and modern scene illustrations have different evidentiary value; none establishes the pictured faces.
