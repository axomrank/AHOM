## Landscape and buildings

Upper Assam: humid floodplain, wet alluvium, reeds, bamboo/cane, rice margins, broadleaf woodland, river mist. Dimapur: Dhansiri valley and low forested foothills, sandstone monoliths in a modest timber/bamboo and earthen enclosure. Some masonry and stone context belongs to Dimasa Dimapur; avoid applying an absolute all-timber rule to that site. Carved pillar placement, building forms and damage are reconstructed. Exclude later brick Ahom palaces and Rang Ghar, Mughal marble forms, modern flags, fantasy heraldry and homogenized tribal costumes.
