# RAM SINGH I — reference and design

Prepared 16 October 2026. Modern artistic reconstruction. See ../mughal-bengal-14-research.md for source access, provenance and limitations. Proposed faces, ages, garment cuts and colours are artistic choices except where explicitly connected to a source portrait description. No individual local scene is treated as an authenticated portrait.

Raja Ram Singh I of Amber, Hindu Kachhwaha Rajput Mughal general around age 40 in Assam 1669–71, not Ram Singh II of Jaipur. Source Cleveland 2013.330.a circa1680–85 shows red turban, moustache, brown fur-collar coat over green patterned tunic and orange trousers, straight sword red scabbard. Adapt this documented later court portrait to a serious forty-year-old man: warm tan brown skin, long oval face, aquiline nose, dark eyes, strong black slightly upward sweeping moustache, clean-shaven chin, tall lean build. Red compact Rajput folded turban with small jewel, muted green subtly floral jama, brown silk honor coat with narrow light fur collar, muted orange close trousers, red-gold patka, restrained pearl/gem necklace, brown gold-edged mojari, straight sword sheathed in red scabbard at side. No generic Hindu crown, beard, massive forehead symbol or fantasy armor. Clothing is portrait-based court ensemble, not asserted Assam battle uniform.
