### 01 · The journey begins (1215)
Dawn in Mong Mao. Prince Sukaphaa, a young Tai man, turns his horse and looks back at his homeland, then rides out at the head of his followers.
**Dialogue (Sukaphaa):** "নতুন দেশ বিচাৰি যাম।" *(I will go and find a new land.)*
**On screen:** চাওলুং সুকাফা · ১২১৫ — যাত্ৰাৰ আৰম্ভণি

### 02 · Crossing the Patkai (1215–1228)
Thick fog over the Patkai hills. Followers and horses climb the ridge. Sukaphaa drives his spear into the summit.
**Dialogue:** "এই পাহাৰৰ সিপাৰেই আমাৰ ভৱিষ্যত।" *(Our future lies beyond these hills.)*
**On screen:** পাটকাই পাৰ হৈ

### 03 · Arrival in Assam (Dec 1228)
Sukaphaa reaches the green valley of Namrup, kneels and lets the soil run through his fingers. Sunrise breaks behind him.
**Dialogue:** "এই মাটিয়েই আমাৰ ঘৰ।" *(This land is our home.)*
**On screen:** অসমলৈ আগমন · ২ ডিচেম্বৰ ১২২৮

### 04 · Building Bor Axom (1230s)
By firelight, Sukaphaa clasps hands with the Moran chief Badaucha and the Barahi chief Thakumatha, and their people gather around.
**Dialogue:** "আজিৰ পৰা আমি সকলো এক।" *(From today, we are all one.)*
**On screen:** বৰ অসমৰ ভেটি

### 05 · Charaideo (1253)
An aged Sukaphaa stands on a misty hilltop, looking over the first capital as it is being built, with lightning far off.
**Dialogue:** "ইয়াতেই হ'ব আমাৰ ৰাজধানী — চৰাইদেউ।" *(Here will be our capital: Charaideo.)*
**On screen:** চৰাইদেউ · ১২৫৩

### 06 · War to alliance (1324)
King Sukhaangphaa's army faces the Kamata army in the rain. Swords lower, and the two Kamata princesses, Rajani and Bhajani, walk forward between the lines.
**Dialogue (Kamata envoy):** "যুদ্ধ নহয়, এতিয়া সম্বন্ধ।" *(No more war. Now, kinship.)*
**On screen:** অহোম–কমতা সন্ধি

### 07 · The empty throne (1364–1397)
A dark royal hall where the throne stands empty. Lightning flashes, and the nobles whisper in the shadows.
**Dialogue (a noble):** "ৰজা নাই… সিংহাসন খালী।" *(No king… the throne is empty.)*
**On screen:** ৰজাবিহীন ৰাজ্য

### 08 · The Brahmin-raised prince (1397)
In a Brahmin household in Habung, a teenage boy is reading. The trader Thao Cheoken stops and stares at him. Cut to the Burhagohain kneeling to the boy at Charaideo.
**Dialogue (Burhagohain):** "এয়াই আমাৰ ৰজা!" *(He is our king!)*
**On screen:** বামুণী কোঁৱৰ সুদাংফা · ১৩৯৭

### 09 · Swarganarayan (1497)
Suhungmung's coronation: the crown is lowered, then a glowing map of the kingdom spreads outward behind him.
**Dialogue (Suhungmung):** "অসমৰ সীমা আৰু বহল হ'ব।" *(Assam's borders will grow wider.)*
**On screen:** স্বৰ্গনাৰায়ণ সুহুংমুং

### 10 · The first victory (1527)
A night battle on the river, with Ahom war boats and fire arrows. Suhungmung raises his sword as the Bengal invaders under Bit Malik retreat.
**Dialogue:** "শত্ৰুক উভতি পঠিয়াই দিয়া!" *(Drive the enemy back!)*
**On screen:** ১৫২৭ · অহোমৰ প্ৰথম বিজয়
