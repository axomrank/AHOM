# Mughal and Bengal group: fourteen character designs

Prepared 16 October 2026. User requested all fourteen catalogue entries. This group spans Bengal Sultanate commanders and seventeenth-century Mughal officers, allied Hindu rulers, an emperor, a prince and a chronicler. Religion alone is not a costume specification.

## Identity safeguards

- Bit Malik and Turbak: pre-Mughal Bengal Sultanate context, individual likeness unknown.
- Satrajit: Bhusna regional ruler; not assumed to be Rajput because Hindu.
- Abu Bakr: 1615 commander, not the early caliph.
- Qasim Khan Chishti: early Jahangir-era subahdar, not later Juvayni namesake. Personal likeness unknown.
- Allah Yar Khan: 1639 frontier/treaty role from catalogue; no personal portrait established.
- Mir Jumla: elderly Mir Jumla II, not an unrelated earlier namesake pictured in c.1619 art.
- Dilir: Daudzai identification is an indexed scholarly lead; no authenticated face.
- Talish: writer and reporter, not armored frontline captain; closed notebook is an interpretive role prop.
- Syed Firoz: Guwahati faujdar entry; no unverified genealogical insignia or portrait.
- Aurangzeb: c.1667 middle-aged appearance; no claim he personally commanded in Assam.
- Ram Singh I: Amber ruler, not nineteenth-century Ram Singh II. Later portrait court clothing explicitly distinguished from battle equipment.
- Munawar: Saraighat catalogue entry; no unrelated later officer portrait copied.
- Muhammad Azam: young prince around1679, not Muazzam/Bahadur Shah; portrait attribution limitations retained.

## Production

Fourteen distinct design briefs and prompts saved in individual research directories. All use eight views, serious expressions, naturalistic studio presentation and ARTISTIC RECONSTRUCTION footer. No exact undocumented face, age, uniform or religious marker is asserted as historical fact. Canonical historical summaries and chronology are preserved; additions concern character sheets and provenance notes.
