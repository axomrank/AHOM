# THAO CHEOKEN TRADER — simple official sheet

User direction: make officers 2–8 in the simplified First Burhagohain / Momai Tamuli style, with different faces.

Local clothing and format references:
- `media/characters/thaomung-burhagohain/turnaround-1.webp` (simplified cream robe version).
- `media/characters/momai-tamuli/turnaround-1.webp`.

Faces are deliberately distinct provisional artistic designs, not authenticated portraits or copies of either reference man.

Face design: Age 43, slender long triangular face, high narrow cheekbones, long slightly arched nose, sparse short moustache, pointed clean-shaven chin, fine smile lines around warm eyes, slim build. NOT a heavy jaw or goatee.

Costume: plain warm beige robe, no embroidery, dusty brown headwrap and narrow umber sash. No sword for this trader.

Built-in image generation. No external research. Eight photorealistic views. Local scene inspection informed the project context, but user-directed simple cream official clothing takes precedence over scene ceremonial or battle costumes.
