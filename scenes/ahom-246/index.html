Balinarayan Takes Refuge at the Ahom Court

1613–1615 · Against the Mughal Empire

After the Mughals defeat Parikshit of Koch Hajo, his brother Balinarayan flees to Pratap Singha, who makes him tributary raja of Darrang as Dharmanarayan. The refuge angers the Mughal governor Qasim Khan.

Painting depicts asylum; Qasim Khan is not physically shown.
