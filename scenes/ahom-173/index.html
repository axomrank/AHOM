Treason of Laluk Sola: Surrender of Guwahati to the Mughals

February 1679 · Six kings in nine years

In an act of monstrous betrayal, Laluk Sola Borphukan surrenders Guwahati to Mughal Prince Azam Tara for 400,000 rupees and promises of being made Raja.
