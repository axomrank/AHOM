The Treaty of Ghilajharighat

January 1663 · Against the Mughal Empire

Severely ill Mir Jumla and exhausted Jayadhwaj sign treaty; Ahoms cede Western Assam, pay war indemnities of gold, silver, 90 elephants, and send princesses.

Sources have not yet been catalogued for this record.

People: Dilir Khan, Jayadhwaj Singha (Sutamla), Mir Jumla
