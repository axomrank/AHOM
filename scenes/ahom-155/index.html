Lachit Rallies Retreating Troops from His Sickbed

March 1671 · Against the Mughal Empire

Suffering from severe high fever, Lachit orders his sickbed carried onto his royal war-boat, shouting to retreating soldiers: 'If you wish to flee, do so! But let the King know I died fighting!'

Sources have not yet been catalogued for this record.

People: Lachit Borphukan, Udayaditya Singha (Sunyatphaa)
