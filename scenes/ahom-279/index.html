Tyao Khamti's Failed Expedition Against the Chutias

1380–1387 · The coming of Sukaphaa

Nandi and Satyanarayana rule the Chutia kingdom from Sadhayapura (Sadiya), the earliest Chutia kings known from inscriptions. Tyao Khamti's campaign against them fails.

The river withdrawal and opposing Chutia ruler are illustrative, not a documented personal confrontation; campaign dates vary.
