Fortification of Saraighat and Execution of the Negligent Uncle

1671 · Against the Mughal Empire

Lachit oversees construction of the Saraighat earthen rampart overnight; finding his maternal uncle negligent, he beheads him uttering: 'My uncle is not greater than my country!'

Sources have not yet been catalogued for this record.
