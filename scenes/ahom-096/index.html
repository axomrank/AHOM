Annexation of the Chutia Kingdom

1523 · Swarganarayan’s kingdom

Chutia forces routed at Chandangiri; Sadiya annexed into the Ahom Empire; office of Sadiya-Khowa Gohain instituted; firearms and copper-plate treasures acquired.

Habung was taken in 1512 and Dhirnarayan routed at Dibru in 1523; by 1524 Phrasengmung Borgohain governed Sadiya.
