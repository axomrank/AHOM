Treaty of the Patkai Boundary at Nongnyang Lake

1401 · The coming of Sukaphaa

Ahom and Mong Kawng envoys swear a treaty by Nongnyang lake fixing the Patkai range as the border between the two kingdoms. The boundary holds for centuries.

Nongnyang lake as the treaty site follows Gait, A History of Assam; Wikipedia gives only the Patkai boundary. Painting depicts the two negotiating commanders; Sudangphaa is not physically shown.
