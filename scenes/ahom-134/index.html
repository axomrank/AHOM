The Monsoon Counter-War of Namrup

June–October 1662 · Against the Mughal Empire

From hills of Namrup, Jayadhwaj unleashes guerrilla warfare during torrential monsoons, severing Mughal supply lines and decimating Mir Jumla's trapped army with epidemic fever.
