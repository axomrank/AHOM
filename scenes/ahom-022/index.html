Institution of Royal Moidams (Pyramidal Earth Tumuli)

1268 onwards · The coming of Sukaphaa

Upon Sukaphaa's demise in 1268 CE, the tradition of building subterranean vaulted brick/stone chambers covered by hemispherical earthen mounds (Moidams) begins at Charaideo.
