Martyrdom of Princess Joymoti Konwari

1680 · Six kings in nine years

Tied to thorn bushes on Jerenga Pathar and brutally tortured for 14 continuous days by Lora Raja's agents, Princess Joymoti breathes her last refusing to betray her husband Gadapani.

Sources have not yet been catalogued for this record.

People: Gadadhar Singha (Supaatphaa), Joymoti Konwari, Sulikphaa (Ratnadhwaj Singha, Lora Roja)
