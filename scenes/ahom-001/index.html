Sukaphaa Departs Mong Mao

1215–1228 · The coming of Sukaphaa

Prince Sukaphaa leaves his ancestral kingdom of Mong Mao following a succession dispute, leading 9,000 followers, noblemen, priests (Deodhais), horses, and two royal war elephants across Upper Burma towards Assam.

Sources have not yet been catalogued for this record.

People: Chaolung Sukaphaa, First Borgohain (Thaomung), First Burhagohain (Thaomung)
